## Overview

Building on the trainability analysis of QCBMs, this research direction explores the
fundamental trade-offs between expressibility and trainability in quantum generative models.

Key aspects of this research:

- **Expressibility quantification** of parameterized quantum circuits
- **Entanglement-driven trainability** analysis
- **Resource estimation** by benchmarking the performance of PQCs according to the number of two-qubit gates, number of parameters, and circuit depth
- **Comparative analysis** of three circuit families: randomly initialized linear-depth circuits, heuristic circuits with varying expressibilities and high circuit depths, and expressibility-enhanced circuits with linear depth

This work has been documented in a published manuscript.
