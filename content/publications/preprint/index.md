abstract: |
  This thesis investigates the trainability of Quantum Circuit Born Machines (QCBMs), a class of quantum generative models. The research focuses on the effects of circuit descriptors like Expressibility and Entanglement Capability on Variational Quantum Circuits. Furthermore, it studies how the choice of loss function affects the trainability and accuracy of these models, ultimately proposing guidelines for designing efficient quantum architectures.
